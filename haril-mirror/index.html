WHAT IS THIS?

A tiny HARIL experiment: the interface treats interaction itself as data. Every press, pause, drag, return, edge visit, and rupture becomes part of a local behavioral trace. That trace continuously alters the graphics, sound, system state, and the observations you receive back.

Nothing is uploaded. The entire piece runs locally in the browser.
The point is recursion. You change the field → the changed field changes what you do next → that changes the field again.
