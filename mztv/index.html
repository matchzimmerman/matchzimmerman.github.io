ABOUT

The stream is the artwork.

MZTV treats broadcast as an artistic medium rather than a distribution endpoint. Browser systems, sound environments, live work, data-driven pieces, transmissions, performances, and experiments can move through the same continuous channel.

The live stream is the primary source. Screens, installations, gallery displays, AR/VR receivers, and recordings are ways of receiving or documenting particular performances of that source.
