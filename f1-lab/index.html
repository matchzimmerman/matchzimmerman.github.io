Two tiny browser tests built from the same race-state model. WINDOW treats strategy as a moving field. PITWALL turns race strategy into a math/data decision game.

01 // Data-art test

WINDOW

Drivers are positioned by strategic state rather than physical track position. Gap, tire age, pace trend, degradation and undercut risk become the visual system.
