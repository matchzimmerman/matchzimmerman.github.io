# SCORE ENGINE 001 — short post text

Paste-ready description for social posts. The full explainer is at
`https://matchzimmerman.com/MZTV/sources/score-engine-001-slo-funk-dub/explainer.html`.

---

Most music visualizers listen to sound and make pictures. This does the opposite. SCORE ENGINE 001 watches a film clip frame by frame (motion, camera moves, cuts, impacts, light, warmth, stillness) and uses those readings to play a slow dub-funk band live. A cut drops the band into an echo and moves the downbeat onto the edit. An impact becomes a kick or rim shot, snapped to the beat. Stillness lets the band step out. It doesn't understand the film; it only measures change. Because films are already edited to a rhythm, the score sometimes lines up as if it were written for the scene.
