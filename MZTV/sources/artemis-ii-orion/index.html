ABOUT THIS STUDY
Artemis II (April 2026) reconstructed from archived NASA/JPL Horizons geometric state vectors for Orion (-1024) and the Moon (301), sampled in an Earth-centered J2000 ecliptic frame. Recorded coordinates and velocities drive position, clock, and distance. Virtual cameras, planet textures, expanded sphere sizes, and musical sonification are artistic, not documentary footage or sound in vacuum.

Audio must be enabled by the viewer. Data timestamps are displayed as approximate UTC (TDB conversion).
