The Inverted Fairground

An abandoned amusement park. A mysterious atmosphere. A mechanical bird. A glowing key. Gravity reverses.

This is a working interactive scene, built from the Mad Lib specification. Explore the fairground, approach the glowing key and press E to change the world's physics, photograph the scene, or watch the automated MZCAM route.

Desktop: WASD or arrows to move · drag to look · E to interact · P to photograph · shift to move faster.
Mobile: Left joystick to move · drag elsewhere to look.

Standalone/offline prototype. PNG export works locally. Browser video recording uses MP4 when supported, otherwise WebM. This is not deployed to a public URL.
