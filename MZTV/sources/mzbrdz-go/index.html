What is actually location-based?

Generated orbs occupy stable seeded GPS locations, not positions that follow your phone. The lime test orb stays at one stored GPS anchor until you press PLACE NEW TEST ANCHOR. Within 90 m you can open your camera and move around an orb; its direction and apparent size follow position and heading changes. GPS accuracy can exceed the apparent size of an orb.

Is this full AR?

Version 002 gives each orb fixed GPS coordinates, projects its bearing/distance relative to phone compass and tilt, and redraws it in the camera as you move. This is geospatial compass AR, not precise ARKit visual-inertial tracking: GPS error, magnetic interference, unknown terrain elevation and camera lens variation can cause drift. Use compass calibration if required.

Privacy and reliability

GPS coordinates are only used on your device to calculate sightings and local anchors. The test anchor remains in local storage. The journal stores an approximate coordinate rounded to 3 decimals, plus photos in IndexedDB. Nothing is automatically uploaded. iPhone browsers may pause GPS/compass updates when screen-locked. Camera, GPS, and motion sensors require browser permission on HTTPS.
