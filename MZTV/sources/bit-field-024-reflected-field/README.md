# MZTV · BIT FIELD 024 · REFLECTED FIELD

A live audiovisual study anchored in reflected light and reflected sound as parallel systems of influence.

The piece does **not** map hue directly to frequency. Instead, light and sound share a path through a common influence graph. Each object has both optical and acoustic material properties. Energy entering the field is transformed at each contact, and the transformed output becomes the input to the next object.

## Interaction

- Click **ENTER REFLECTED FIELD** to enable Web Audio.
- Move the pointer / touch to move the listener through the field.
- Click or press **SPACE** to inject a stronger energy pulse.
- Press **D** to toggle the direct source, allowing a reflections-only listening mode.
- Press **I** to toggle the explanatory text.

## Conceptual anchor

> An object is not perceived independently of the field of influence surrounding it.

The visual color of each object gradually becomes a record of the energy that has reached it. The sonic event at the same object is filtered through the same material encounter, then perceived according to the listener's position. Repeated reflection creates a decaying memory of where a signal has been.
