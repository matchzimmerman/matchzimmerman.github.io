ORIGINAL STORY / FOURTH-GRADE WRITER

The Day I Found the Beauty of Nature

This woodland is an adaptation of the author's experience of noticing one unusual tree on a school hike.

“The trees were like big ice cream cones around us.”
“The sun was like a lemon snowball.”
“The tree was like a giant thick piece of black licorice.”
“Even though I’m just someone small in a big world I am important. Just like the tree.”

The original story and cover drawing are preserved as private reference materials in the project ZIP. The world does not add narration or invented events to the child's writing.
