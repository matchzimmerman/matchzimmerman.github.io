Enter a constructed six-ink world of Spruce Creek, two cascades, and the stone steps between them. Walk from the upper overlook toward the middle pool and descend the 200-step staircase, or surrender to an autonomous camera survey.

FIELD STUDY 001. The drop heights and staircase count follow NYSDEC documentation. The initial terrain and trail geometry are interpretive, not yet derived from georeferenced 3DEP elevation data. Weather is live when available; flow is a visualization, not a measured stream gauge.
