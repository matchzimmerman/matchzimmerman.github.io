02 / Inquiry — questions within the fiction and within the practice

What does a field station observe?

MAGPIE asks the visitor to reconstruct a world from partial evidence. The work investigates how technological systems affect memory, classification, agency, and what can be perceived.

INQUIRY INSIDE MAGPIE

How do systems of observation and control shape the world people are able to perceive?

Within the fiction, environmental signals, documents, devices and spatial clues are interpreted through the competing logics of The Grid and The Field. The viewer's own reconstruction becomes part of the encounter.

EXPLORE THE FICTIONAL WORLD ↗

INQUIRY ABOUT MAKING MAGPIE

What happens when physical, narrative, computational and archival systems shape a single evolving world?

MAGPIE crosses installation, sound, speculative fiction, procedural systems, interfaces and ongoing game development. Different outputs can stand alone while remaining evidence of a larger constructed system.
