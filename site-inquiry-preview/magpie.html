About MAGPIE

Set in 2147, FIELD STATION: MAGPIE is a fictional world developed through physical installation, sound, images, documents, objects and interactive environments.

Exhibition

Physical installation and archival displays at Tuttle Gallery.

World

The Grid, The Field, and the systems of surveillance and control that shape 2147.

Story

Characters and narrative fragments encountered through the world and its artifacts.

Archive

Images, objects, documents and simulations from the evolving project.

Project inquiry

Surveillance, memory, classification, and life beyond systems of control.

Process

MAGPIE connects installation, sound, moving image, writing and interactive systems in one evolving fictional world.
