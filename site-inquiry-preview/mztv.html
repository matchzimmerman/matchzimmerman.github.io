02 / Inquiry — why this body of work exists

What can live systems reveal?

MZTV asks what happens when computational works run, change, and can be observed over time rather than being represented by a single finished image or recording.

INQUIRY INSIDE MZTV

How do forces, data, behavior, sound and observation shape what a system becomes?

Different works examine that through weather, real race telemetry, ecological behavior, long-duration growth, autonomous cameras and audiovisual response. Their questions are not identical, but the broadcast brings them into a shared field.

INQUIRY ABOUT THE PRACTICE

How does human-directed creative work change as tools and outputs become infrastructure for new work?

MZTV is one site where systems are built, tested through sensory experience, revised through human interpretation, and reused in subsequent projects. It makes the evolving practice observable.
