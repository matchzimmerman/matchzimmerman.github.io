02 / Inquiry — what the botanical system investigates

Can the history of a practice
be experienced as a living thing?

48 PLANT translates documented creative activity into environmental conditions felt by the specimen across its forty-eight-hour life.

INQUIRY INSIDE THE WORK

How does accumulated influence become form?

Life records, seeds, activity events and simulated weather change the conditions through which a plant develops. Growth retains its history in leaves, stems, markings and scars.

INQUIRY ABOUT THE PRACTICE

How can creative work become a source of conditions for new creative work?

Here the data of the practice enters the artwork as environmental input. The piece itself then contributes new code, archived media and reusable camera logic to later projects.

The code and source documentation support the existence of these mechanisms; the phrasing of these two questions is provisional and intended for review.
