02 / inquiry about the creative practice

What happens to human thought when more cognition exists outside the human—and what remains uniquely ours when it does?

Across more than four years of distributed creative practice, MZCMG studies how ideas move through humans, tools, artifacts, computation, and physical experience.

The live systems are not illustrations of theory added afterward. They are sites where questions can be tested and new questions can emerge.

SEE AN ACTUAL SYSTEM RELATIONSHIP →

Complex systems of influence

Different forces act on a system over time: weather, commits, camera decisions, terrain, sound, feedback, and prior states. Those relationships become materials for both the artwork and its documentation.

SEE HOW 48 PLANT USES THIS ↗

Human-directed, not personified

Computational systems extend what can be built, observed, remembered, and modified. Creative direction, interpretation and responsibility remain with the human practitioner; the machine is not treated as a peer.

SEE THE MZTV INQUIRY ↗

Experiencing before refining

Ideas become playable simulations, live audiovisual environments, spatial encounters and prototypes. The maker can experience the result, respond to it, and direct the next iteration through embodied feedback.

EXPERIENCE MAGPIE ↗

Evidence, not just outcomes

Originals, versions, conversations, files, artifacts and technical dependencies can form an inspectable history. The public site can give visitors an approachable view without discarding deeper provenance.

04 / evidence of a distributed practice

When one system
makes another possible.

A documented dependency that goes beyond theme or aesthetic similarity: camera logic emerging from 48 PLANT is extracted into MZCam, then used in other environments.

48 PLANT → MZCam → TRACKSIDE / SHEEPDOG

The original artwork develops a need: cameras that can observe and broadcast an evolving organism. The resulting camera logic becomes a reusable module.

That module then supports the presentation of racing telemetry in TRACKSIDE and animal behavior in SHEEPDOG. Each new work places the system under different constraints and gives it additional possibilities.

This is one concrete lineage, not a claim that all creative development follows a single linear path.
