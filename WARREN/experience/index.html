WARREN / FIELD NOTES

This is an experiential blockout of a documented historical settlement—not a verified geographic reconstruction or a current-day map.

The relative arrangement of the river corridor, three road alignments and five village building zones derives from a hand-extracted, unregistered detail of the 1915 Bromley Atlas. Their scale and orientation are not surveyed.

The five-story mill is historically documented. Its form, placement in this world, cottages, bridge, trees, hills, water elevation and the figures you may encounter are provisional interpretations. No modern lidar or underwater bathymetry is presented as recovered pre-inundation terrain.
