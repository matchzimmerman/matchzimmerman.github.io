COMPOSITIONAL MEMORY

The original Walsh pattern becomes a recurring seed. Under autonomous control, new harmonics, pulse ornaments, filtering, echoes and reverberant space accumulate across the arc. The collapse deliberately restores the original motif, with nearly everything else removed.
