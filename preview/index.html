Match Zimmerman Creative Media Group is the artist-led studio and research practice of Match Zimmerman.

BALTIMORE, MARYLAND / ART · SOUND · SYSTEMS · RESEARCH · LEARNING

Match Zimmerman is a multidisciplinary artist, designer, educator, and researcher working across sound, moving image, code, human–AI systems, archives, physical interfaces, and speculative environments.

His work includes exhibitions, moving-image and sound projects, experimental software, teaching, commissioned media, and ongoing research into how people orient themselves inside complex technological systems.
