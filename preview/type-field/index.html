A · keys → chords

  • Top row melody, D4 up the mode, left to right. 9/0 shift its octave.
  • Home row chords. F and J are home (i). Left hand steps down from F, right hand steps up from J. Hold several to stack harmony.
  • Bottom row bass, D2 up the mode.
  • Space strums whatever chords you hold, with a kick. With nothing held it is a drum hit.
  • 1–7 rotate the whole board to another mode of the same seven notes (sections). Nothing goes out of key.
  • Enter Phrygian cadence ♭II → i. ⌫ chokes everything.
  • 8 unlocks the ♯ layer: Shift raises notes a semitone and flips chord quality (Shift+F = D major).

B · words → phrases

  • Space ends the word: its letters become a voiced chord, strummed with a kick and held as a pad, and the word echoes back as a figure on the 112 grid.
  • Typing speed sets velocity, strum tightness and hi-hat density.
  • Bigrams pick the harmony (table below). Otherwise the word's first letter picks the chord.
