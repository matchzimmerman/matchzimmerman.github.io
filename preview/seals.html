TO SEE WHAT SEALS SEE

Looking at what remains inaccessible.

A photographic and poetic body of work using Ireland, looking, memory, family, distance, and language as overlapping forms of observation. Photographs and poems operate as independent records that can distort, contradict, and reframe one another.
