How it plays

  • Gems ride toward you down ten home-row lanes. Type the key as a gem crosses the green line.
  • Every key you press sounds its chord, hit or not. It is an instrument first. Hits score; nothing sounds wrong.
  • The full-width green bar is Space, the downbeat: a kick drum (in Soleá, the golpe tap). If you are holding chord keys when you hit it, it plays them again with the kick.
  • F and J have amber bumps, same as your keyboard. The song starts there and walks outward.
  • Top row plays melody and bottom row plays bass, so you can jam over the chart.
  • Enter start · Esc stop · 1/2 difficulty · 3 view · 4 track while idle.
  • Views for comparing: letters puts the letter on each tile in its lane; stream drops the lanes so the letter is the only cue; shapes is blank gems, position only.

Sync

Hits are timed against the audio you hear, from the key event's own timestamp. If you keep landing early or late, play a section, then press auto-calibrate. It moves the offset by your average error.
