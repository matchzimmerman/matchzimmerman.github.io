## Grammar

- Printed matter and a functioning instrument at once. Cream paper, navy/blue structures, teal for contrast, coral accents, ochre sparingly.
- Text is functional and compact. JetBrains Mono, sensible readable sizes for the website; register-style small caps/labels and precise rules; no generic AI dashboard decoration.
- Directional linocut-like hatching is a *visual property of HEADLAND itself*. The site may echo it in small graphic treatments, not cover the actual artwork with a fake texture layer.
- Homepage is a **plain index** with direct entry points to actual work. Work first. Minimal introduction. Clear categories, responsive columns, no autoplay of multiple heavy engines.
- Individual artworks retain their own formal systems. Do not recolor original thumbnails or force all projects into this colorway.
- No image generation. Art images must be source captures or original assets, with grounded provenance.
- This source image is an approved **art-direction reference**. Its inclusion as a public site asset is not implicit; preserve the actual source unchanged.
