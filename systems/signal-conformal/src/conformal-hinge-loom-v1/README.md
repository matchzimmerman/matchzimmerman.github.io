# Conformal Hinge Loom v1

Manual, conversation-tested animation iteration in the `signal-conformal` family.

## Form

A single black line on a warm paper field splits into 3, 7, and 11 traces. A traveling compression aperture folds the traces through a local hinge. The field then undergoes a large oval/ring morph before settling into a reduced residual bundle.

## Sound

Stereo low-sine composition in a D-minor/Dorian/suspended field: centered sub roots, tuned bass-round answers, low kick and tom pressure, restrained upper sine traces, no snare, no hand claps.

## Render

- 480 × 480
- 4 fps
- 66 seconds
- H.264 MP4 + AAC stereo audio
- Pillow, NumPy, FFmpeg
