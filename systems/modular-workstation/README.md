# FIELD STATION: MAGPIE — Interference Field Instrument

This package contains the approved procedural direction developed from the persistent phase-field studies.

The instrument does not illustrate objects. It runs families of rotating vectors whose local relationships produce temporary consensus. Rings, folds, braids, volumes, tunnels, and other recognizable structures may appear, but they are never rendered as separate high-resolution graphics. They surface briefly through synchronization and dissolve back into the field.

## Locked visual grammar

- spinning lines remain the primary and only resolved graphic language;
- rhythm is more important than geometry;
- multiple slow rotational frequencies create beats and rare reinforcement;
- structures must remain incomplete, unstable, and perceptually inferred;
- no separate crystal, model, hero object, or high-resolution reveal;
- amber is reserved for `FIELD STATION: MAGPIE` and rare system events;
- the station title behaves like phosphor burned into the instrument;
- CRT behavior belongs to the signal itself, not to a decorative overlay;
- persistent memory allows earlier agreements to haunt later states.

## Conceptual frame

The artwork is the relationship between a hidden system and a human nervous system trying to understand it. Anticipation, locking, withholding, fracture, haunting, recovery, and release emerge from dynamics rather than conventional representation.

Sound and image should ultimately come from the same oscillators and coupling rules. The goal is not audio-reactivity, but one phenomenon observed through two senses.

See `BEHAVIOR-AS-NARRATIVE.md` for the larger research and production framework.
