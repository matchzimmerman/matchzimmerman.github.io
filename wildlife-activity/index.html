READ THIS CORRECTLY

Reports are not a risk score.

No recent reports found does not mean no animals are present. Citizen-science observations are uneven, some locations are obscured, records can arrive late, and GBIF may contain records also sourced from iNaturalist. This prototype therefore keeps sources separate and treats official alerts as a distinct layer.
