1. Open this page in Safari.

If you're inside the ChatGPT browser, use its menu or Share control to open the page in Safari. No ZIP, USDZ download, or Files app is needed.

2. Tap PLACE IN AR ON iPHONE.

The viewer automatically creates a USDZ from this GLB and opens Apple's AR Quick Look. In Quick Look, select AR if it starts in Object mode.

3. At Foxleigh's site, scan the ground.

Stand a safe distance from where you want the mansion, pan the phone slowly to detect a surface, then move/rotate/pinch to align the model manually.

4. Walk around carefully.

ARKit should hold the object in place better than the browser GPS sketch, but tracking can still fail if the surroundings lack visible features.

Important: This is a model placement test, not historically located GPS AR. The building's dimensions, geometry, façades and rotation remain hypothetical. A mansion-scale model may open very large or initially scaled down: use the AR viewer's gestures and choose an unobstructed outdoor area. Don't look at the screen while walking near obstacles or traffic.
