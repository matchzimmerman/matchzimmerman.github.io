The system should show that it hears you.

The Organic Bit field is not a logo. It is perceptual evidence: voice energy deforms it, interpretation reorganizes it, and intent causes it to materialize files, notes, images, and documents inside the same conversational space.

Prototype thesis

Conversation is the organizing layer. Apps become capabilities that surface only when intent requires them.

Organic Bit states

idle / ambient
hearing / audio-reactive
interpreting / re-forming
materializing / artifact

What is real

Microphone amplitude, browser speech recognition where available, local file/image drops, local note persistence, and the visual state machine.

What is speculative

Cloud Photos, Drive, Calendar, messages, and other service connections are mocked. No external account access is implied.

Mic audio drives the field locally in your browser. Service cards are speculative. Dropped files are not uploaded.
